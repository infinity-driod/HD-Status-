Technical details (for troubleshooting) 

        
      

    

  


  


  

  

    
Paste a link, or pick a video or audio file. Snipr finds the song name, and gives you the sound as an MP3 and the video as an MP4 when it can. Recognising a song sends a short sample (about 20 seconds) of its sound to a recognition service.

    

      
      
    

    
    
    

    

  

  


  

    Song recognition (advanced)
    
A song's name is read from the post's own sound label when there is one (TikTok). To recognise a song by listening, the app uses the song relay set in config.json (for everyone). Here you can paste a relay address to test one, or an AudD token to use on this phone only when no relay is set.

    
    
  


  

  

    
Pick a video. Snipr looks at it and picks the best settings by itself. Long videos are cut into 30-second parts.
